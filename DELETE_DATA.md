# Doodle Duel data deletion requests

Doodle Duel: Draw & Guess is published by KANDIBOINA JNANADEEP. You can request deletion of information held by the game's moderation service without creating a GitHub account.

## How to request deletion

1. Email [kandiboinajnanadeep@gmail.com](mailto:kandiboinajnanadeep@gmail.com?subject=Doodle%20Duel%20data%20deletion%20request) with the subject **Doodle Duel data deletion request**.
2. Include the display name you used and, if you know them, the approximate dates of play, room code, or guest identifier. Do **not** send your guest secret or password. We may ask for additional information privately to locate the correct records and prevent deletion of another player's data.
3. We will review the request and reply with the outcome. If you cannot use email, you may also [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/VRVikramAditya243/doodle-duel-privacy/issues/new), but do not post private details in a public issue.

## What can be deleted or retained

Clearing Doodle Duel's storage in Android settings removes local profile, preferences, guest identifier and secret, and local progress from that device. Uninstalling the app also removes local app storage in the usual Android manner. A request to us can remove the guest registration and related server-held moderation information that we can identify, subject to information we must temporarily retain for an active safety investigation or legal obligation. Report evidence is normally removed after 30 days, and moderation-decision audit records after 180 days. Guest identifiers and current warning or suspension records can remain until a deletion request is processed. Hosting logs and backups may have different retention periods.

A request to us cannot remove information another player has copied, or directly delete records held independently by Photon or Google. For information about Google's advertising data controls, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For the full game policy, see the [Doodle Duel Privacy Policy](https://github.com/VRVikramAditya243/doodle-duel-privacy/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
